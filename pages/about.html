About Harrison Handmade

Crafting premium leather goods with passion and precision

Our Story

Harrison Handmade was founded on the belief that quality leather goods should be accessible to everyone. What started as a personal passion for leather craftsmanship has evolved into a full-fledged business dedicated to creating premium, handcrafted leather products.

Each piece we create is meticulously handcrafted using traditional techniques combined with modern design sensibilities. We believe in the importance of supporting local craftsmanship and using sustainable, high-quality materials.

Our Commitment

  • ✓ Quality Materials: We source only the finest leather from trusted suppliers who share our commitment to quality and sustainability.
  • ✓ Handcrafted Excellence: Every product is individually crafted with meticulous attention to detail and care.
  • ✓ Durability: Built to last a lifetime, our leather goods improve with age and develop unique character over time.
  • ✓ Customer Service: We stand behind every product and are committed to your satisfaction.

Leather Care

Our leather products are designed to age gracefully. With proper care, they will develop a beautiful patina and continue to serve you for years to come.

Care Tips:

  • Avoid prolonged exposure to water. If wet, allow to air dry naturally.
  • Clean with a soft, damp cloth and mild soap as needed.
  • Condition periodically with leather conditioner to maintain suppleness.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
  • The natural patina that develops over time is a feature, not a flaw—it adds character and beauty.
